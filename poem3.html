Canto III • Cosmic Balance

The Dance of the Sun & Moon

The Sun brings life, warmth, and fire,
The Moon reflects the starlight high,
Different realms of light and grace,
Dancing forever across the same sky.

Though daylight fades and shadows fall,
Their quiet gravity holds it all.
Under the sun or beneath the star,
I will always love you as you are. 💫
